# Pocket Pier — Art Direction and High-Leverage Asset Plan

## Style lock: Cozy Cut-Paper Coastal

Pocket Pier uses a polished 2D storybook look built from soft, rounded silhouettes, subtle paper grain and restrained cel shading. Shapes stay readable at phone size. The world is warm and handcrafted rather than glossy, realistic or overloaded.

- Palette: sea-glass turquoise, deep harbor blue, sun-cream, coral orange, warm cedar and small gold accents.
- Rendering: 2D painted cut-paper layers, 2–3 value steps per object, soft texture, crisp outer silhouettes.
- Perspective: side-on gameplay with a slight elevated view of the water.
- Characters: charming proportions, large readable gestures, no facial detail smaller than a few pixels.
- UI: cream cards, navy type, coral primary actions, rounded corners and minimal ornament.
- FX: graphic rings, droplets and stars; short, readable and never particle-heavy.
- Avoid: photorealism, pixel art, generic 3D mobile-game gloss, neon casino effects, excessive gradients, tiny detail and visual noise.

## Ten assets with the greatest leverage

1. `bg_sunny_pier.png` — establishes most of the screen, atmosphere and perceived quality.
2. `character_angler.png` — gives the core action personality and makes every input legible.
3. `gear_rod_basic.png` — visually connects cast, hook and reel states.
4. `fish_minnow_hero.png` — makes the first catch feel like a creature rather than a data result.
5. `fish_sardine_hero.png` — creates immediate catch variation without widening systems scope.
6. `ui_cast_meter.png` — makes the first interaction instantly understandable.
7. `ui_reel_panel.png` — clarifies player marker, fish target and catch progress.
8. `fx_water_splash.png` — sells lure landing, bite and escape with one reusable effect.
9. `fx_perfect_hook.png` — supplies the sharp skill-reward moment the prototype lacks.
10. `ui_catch_card.png` — turns the reward into a memorable reveal and supports rarity variants.

## Integration order

Background and character first, then the visible rod/line/bobber chain. Next integrate fish and reusable effects. Finish with the three gameplay/reward UI assets. No collection, quest or upgrade content is added until one complete catch feels good.
